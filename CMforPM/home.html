HOW DOES IT WORK?

(For existing users)

01

Login Yourself

Login with your Email/Student ID and Password.

02

Update Profile

Enter your correct email-id, mobile number and save your details.

03

Report or Search

Post a found item with photo, location, and details—or search existing listings.

04

Claim Items

Submit a claim with justification. Finders can ask verification questions.

05

Chat Securely

Use the private chat to coordinate pickup while keeping contact info private.

06

Resolve & Close

Once returned, mark resolved. Admins can moderate and handle disputes.
